## Scope

Whole 4MED app, one world: landing and pricing (Persuade), doctor signup and staff join, patient portal (directory, booking, payment proof, consent signing, My Appointments), Clinic App (dashboard, patients, appointments/queue, billing, doctor portal and profession charts, practice settings, admin), and the printed documents. Primary mode Operate; the landing is Persuade inside the same world.

Audience and job: Philippine private practices (practitioners and front desk) running their day; patients on phones booking, paying up front and signing consent; practitioners evaluating plans. Copy rule (user-confirmed 2026-10-18): rewrite freely, drop unproven claims (no "40% fewer no-shows", "HIPAA-ready", "AES-256", "bank-grade"); add no new claims.

Must not feel: cold and sterile, generic SaaS, playful, or dense.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every booking is a clinic card, the tinted appointment card and queue stub a Philippine clinic secretary fills in and rubber-stamps. Refuses the white-and-blue rounded-card SaaS dashboard and the old dark neon "futuristic" look.

OWN-WORLD: Light ground #F2F3EF, paper #FFFFFF, one navy ink #13305E (from the logo), card-stock tints as flat unmodulated fields owning regions: sky #CFE3F5 (doctor), mint #CDEBD9 (dentist), butter #F6E7A8 (ophthalmologist), lilac #DDD5F0 (psych). Violet stamp-pad ink #5B3FA8 for state; void red #B8322F. Archivo only (raise from Design Annual Plates: one grotesque at every size), condensed widths for queue numbers and stamps, tabular figures. 1px ink hairlines are the only dividers; small 6–10px corners; no gradients, glows or glass (raise from Zoo Guide Map). Dashed perforation lines split stubs from cards.

STORY: Staff see today's queue and what needs doing; patients see their card, what is paid, what is signed, and their number; evaluators see the real card and how the day runs before the pricing.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Clinic App: profession-tinted rail with logo, clinic name and modules; header with date and clinic hours, primary actions top right; a hairline strip of today's counts; the queue as a fixed-column board ranked by time (raise from Split-Flap: rows change state in place), large condensed tabular queue numbers left, states as angled violet stamps right; "Now serving" stub at right with tear line. Landing: headline left, a live clinic card at right being stamped Paid → Checked in → Signed.

FORM: Clinic Card, candidate 6 of 7 on my ordered list; seed:5d8de115d4680bca. Signature move: the rubber stamp, a state change lands as an angled ink impression (scale-down press, slight rotation, ink settle), used for Paid, Checked in, Confirmed, Signed, Completed, Cancelled. Raises also kept: fixed-scale charts across visits (Botanical Folio); directory details open on focus while neighbours quiet (Streaming Title Wall).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
